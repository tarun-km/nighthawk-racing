          Grand Prix · You vs 3 rivals

          Three laps against the CPU. Start from the back and fight through. Drive through Hawk Boxes for power-ups and fire them with E. Hold a drift with Space and let go for a mini-turbo. Sit close behind a rival to catch the slipstream. Win on Pro to unlock Legend.
